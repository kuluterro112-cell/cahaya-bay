Cahaya Bay: a complete 3D open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, playable offline in a desktop browser. Explore a sun-soaked coastal city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the police, and play six story missions. Built with three.js, all original.

## The game

You are Raka, a former street racer back in Cahaya Bay after five years away, pulled back into crime to save his father's garage from the Laskar Senja gang. The city has five districts — Marina, Old Town, Downtown, Cahaya Beach and Green Hills — with traffic, pedestrians, traffic lights, a day-night cycle, and the sea at its edge.

- On foot and by car: walk, run, jump, steal parked and NPC cars, drive four vehicle types
- Wanted level 1–5 stars: foot police, patrol cars, roadblocks and a helicopter
- Six story missions with briefings, checkpoints, rewards and a final raid
- Combat: fists and a pistol, gang enemies with health bars, ammo/health/armor pickups
- HUD with a live minimap, speedometer, wanted stars and mission objectives
- Synthesized WebAudio sound: engine, sirens, gunshots and three synthwave radio stations
- Save and continue via localStorage

## Controls

WASD move / drive · mouse camera · Shift run · Space jump / handbrake · E enter-exit car & interact · F or left click punch · left click shoot (when armed) · 1/2 switch weapon · R reload · H horn · L headlights · Q change radio station · M mute · Esc pause.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
